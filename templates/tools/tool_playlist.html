How to Use This Tool — Documentation & Features

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Enter a custom title for your playlist in the top field.
  2. Enter your track names separated by commas or new lines in the text box.
  3. Click Generate M3U8 & Schema to create indexed track metadata and raw #EXTM3U playlist text.

Key Capabilities & Features

  • M3U8 Export Generation: Produces standard playlist directives compatible with VLC, Foobar2000, and mobile players.
  • Automated Track Indexing: Formats track numbers and filenames into clean, lossless schema structures.
